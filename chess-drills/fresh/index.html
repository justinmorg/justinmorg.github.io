P

Priority — hanging material

The single biggest source of thrown-away wins, and the reason this set sits ahead of the endgame drills. Across the 220 blitz games of 19–31 August 2026 there are 34 middlegame moves you played while already winning by +1.5 or more, where a capture worth at least a pawn and a half was on the board and you played something else. They split into two different skills, so they're now two different drills.

Group A — the scan. 22 positions where the threat was already there before you moved. The question is answerable from the position alone: which of your squares is loose? Give yourself fifteen seconds, name the square out loud, then open the answer. 79% have exactly one; the rest have two. This is the one to run now.

Group B — the check before you commit. 12 positions where nothing was hanging until your move created it. The board shows the position before you moved and the move is named underneath — picture it, then find the punishment. Unique answer every time. Do these after A.

Neither group is played out against the computer. Fifteen seconds, name it, move on — the skill is the scan, not the calculation.
