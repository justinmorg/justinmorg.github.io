How to run one
  1. Tap Play it out. The Lichess board opens with the position loaded, oriented your way, you to move.
  2. Board menu (☰ under the board) → Continue from here → Play with the computer.
  3. Set the level listed and convert it.
  4. Won it clearly twice? Tick it off. Lost it twice? Tap Review the real game — you'll usually find you played your original move again.

Put a clock on it — 5+0, same as your real games. Your conversion problem and your clock problem are the same problem, and an untimed drill trains only half of it.

Level 3 (≈1400) is your mirror. Level 4 (≈1700) is the stretch. Levels are set per position — don't go higher; you'd lose for reasons that have nothing to do with technique.

Order is C → B → A → D, top to bottom. Group D last, and read its note first.
